## What this is
Year-indexed map of ICT concepts on youtube.com/@innercircletrader (2013–2026). Study aid, not a trading system and not primary source.

## Eight families (shared vocabulary for ATLAS)
1. Market structure — BOS, MSS, CISD, dealing range, IRL/ERL
2. Liquidity — BSL/SSL, EQH/EQL, PDH/PDL, inducement, Turtle Soup, DOL
3. Imbalance / PD arrays — FVG, IFVG, BPR, CE, NDOG/NWOG, First Presented FVG
4. Order-flow blocks — OB, Breaker, Mitigation, Rejection, Propulsion, Hidden OB, Suspension
5. Time & sessions — Killzones, Macros, Asian Range, Judas, PO3/AMD, Silver Bullet, Opening Range, IPDA
6. Confluence — SMT, OTE, Premium/Discount, Mean Threshold
7. Named models — MMXM, 2022 Model, Silver Bullet, Unicorn, OSOK, CRT, Venom, Charter PAM
8. Risk & process — fixed fractional, structural stops, journaling, no live during Phase 1

## Canonical delivery chain (document’s hierarchy)
HTF bias → draw on liquidity → killzone/macro → liquidity raid → displacement / MSS or CISD → PD array (usually first presented FVG or OB) in premium/discount → opposing liquidity.

Named models = packaged versions of that chain. Later years mostly refine time-boxing, not new physics.
