## 4. Required sample (power)
Trade-level OOS data: N=244, mean m=0.227R, SD s=2.170R, which is heavily right-skewed. Normal-approximation N for a two-sided CI lower bound > 0, assuming the true effect equals m and the variance is unchanged:

| Target | Formula | N |
|---|---|---|
| Lower 95% bound > 0 **at the point estimate** (~50% power) | (1.96·s/m)² | **351** |
| 80% power, α=0.05 two-sided | ((1.96+0.84)·s/m)² | **717** |
| 80% power, α=0.025 (Bonferroni across H013 + H014, §5) | ((2.24+0.84)·s/m)² | **869** |
| Smallest mean detectable at the Wave 1 floor N=80 | 1.96·s/√80 | 0.476R (2.1× the observed effect) |

- **Wave 1 gate N ≥ 80 is kept, not amended.** It is the minimum before any FAILS/SURVIVES language. At N=80 a SURVIVES needs an observed mean ≥ ~0.48R, so the honest expectation at 80 is **INCONCLUSIVE** unless the effect is much larger than the OOS estimate.
- **Proposed decision N (forward): N_fwd = 870** v1-base US100 trades (80% power at the Bonferroni α). Interim looks at N = 80, 240, 480 are allowed **for futility/kill only** (§5). No early SURVIVES.
- Trade rate: 244/258 ≈ 0.95 trades/day, so 80 trades ≈ 4 months, 351 ≈ 17 months and 870 ≈ 3.6 years of forward sessions. Because of that horizon, see HIGHEST-VALUE NEXT (an unseen-history holdout).
- **Forward sample definition:** the count starts at the first NY session **after** this lock is finalized (CASSANDRA + DATA). The 28 Sep–2 Oct 2026 forward rows are pre-lock, on the wrong feed and partly backfilled, so they are logged and **not counted**.

## 5. Pass/fail criteria (fixed before any counted outcome)
Primary estimand: mean R_5050 per trade after costs, US100 v1 base, frozen engine, on the DATA-approved Dukascopy BID 1m feed.

**SURVIVES (all required, evaluated only at N_fwd ≥ 870; a lower N only if CASSANDRA amends the decision N *before* the first counted session):**
1. Expectancy after costs ≥ **+0.20 R/trade** (the rules-file bar).

3. Random-entry benchmark (same days, same stop/target R-multiples, random side and minute 09:30–10:59, 1000 runs, per `random_benchmark.csv` method) > 95th percentile.
4. Tail-robustness: expectancy excluding the top 2% of trades by R stays > 0.
5. Drawdown and streak inside the OOS-implied envelope: forward max DD (R) ≤ the bootstrap p95 for the realized N (N=80: **23.2R**, 360: **38.9R**, 720: **46.0R**; ORION bootstrap of OOS trades, 4,000 draws) **and** the longest losing streak ≤ the bootstrap p99 (N=80: 16, 360: 19, 720: 21).
6. Costs: expectancy at 2× costs > 0.

**FAILS (any, checked at each interim look ≥ 80):**
- 95% CI upper bound < +0.20R (the edge is excluded at the bar), **or**
- the max-DD or losing-streak envelope in item 5 is breached, **or**
- at N_fwd ≥ 240, cumulative forward expectancy ≤ 0.

## 6. Paper risk limits (apply to any forward test; RISK hard caps, `risk/GATES.md`)
- Equity $100,000 (paper) · **0.5%** max risk per trade ($500) · **2%** max daily loss ($2,000) · **5%** max drawdown ($5,000).
- **RISK flag:** at 0.5%/trade the OOS max DD of **18.0R = 9.0%** of equity and the 10-trade losing streak (≈ −10.3R ≈ −5.2%) both breach the 5% cap. Bootstrap P(max DD ≥ 10R within the first 80 trades) ≈ **0.67**. A capped paper book would very likely halt before N=80 and truncate the research sample.
- Draft resolution for RISK/CASSANDRA: the research estimand is logged in **R-units as a shadow log** (no orders). A paper account, if any, is sized ≤ 0.25%/trade (18R → 4.5%) and halts at the 5% cap without stopping the R-unit log.
- Daily loss: one trade/day/instrument ≈ 1.05R ≈ 0.52% at 0.5%. That is within 2%, including US500 LOG-ONLY.
