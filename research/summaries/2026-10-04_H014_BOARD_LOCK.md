3. Random-entry baseline (same days/sides/risk/R-targets, random 1m bar 09:30–10:58, the `mmxm/baseline.py` method, 5,000 draws) p < 0.05 on the forward sample.
4. **Cross-instrument (selection-consistent):** forward expectancy > 0 on **both** US100 and US500. The pre-registered v5 selection rule already required IS > 0 on both (`selection_v5.json`), so this is a criterion here, not just a scope note.
5. Drawdown and streak inside the envelope: forward max DD ≤ the bootstrap p95 of all 48 trades at the realized N (N=80: **6.3R**; 212: **7.6R**) **and** the longest losing streak ≤ p99 (N=80: 8; 212: 9).

- the item-5 DD or streak envelope is breached, **or**
